AI/ML Architecture & Optimization Pipeline

College Engineering Laboratory Project Reference

1. Data Ingestion: Historical lab consumption logs stored in SQLite are extracted into Pandas DataFrames for feature engineering (lag features, rolling averages, semester examination cycle indicators).

2. Demand Forecasting: Regression models (Scikit-learn Linear Regression, Polynomial Trend) forecast upcoming component requirements ahead of practicals and student projects.

3. Inventory Optimization: Computes statistical Safety Stock ($SS = Z \cdot \sigma_d \cdot \sqrt{L}$) and Reorder Point ($ROP = \bar{d} \cdot L + SS$) to prevent shortages while minimizing holding costs.

4. Anomaly Detection: Statistical Z-score evaluation identifies abnormal component loss, hoarding, or unexpected usage spikes exceeding $2.0 \sigma$.
